Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

NNK Freight Inc ("NNK," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit nnkfreightinc.com, call or text us, request a quote, or apply to drive with us; how we use and protect it; and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you contact us, request a quote, book a shipment, apply to drive, or text or call us, we may collect your name, company name, email address, phone number (including mobile number), shipment details, and any other information you choose to share. Driver applicants may also provide CDL information, employment history, and driving record details needed for the hiring process.

Information collected automatically

When you browse our website, basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited may be recorded by our website hosting provider in standard server logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your questions, quote requests, and job applications
  • To provide, manage, and improve our transportation services
  • To send updates about your shipments, pickups, deliveries, or your application, including by text message if you have opted in
  • To verify driver qualifications and employment history as required for hiring
  • To keep our website secure and understand how it is used
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations, including U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) and FMCSA requirements

3. Text Messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to receive text messages from NNK Freight Inc, we use your mobile number only to send you conversational messages related to your shipments, driver application, or inquiries. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for help. Full details are in our SMS Terms & Conditions.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers: companies that help us run our business, such as website hosting and phone or messaging services, only for the purposes we direct and subject to confidentiality obligations. This does not include sharing mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data for marketing purposes, as stated above.
  • Shipment partners: the shipment details needed to complete a load (for example, pickup and delivery contacts) with the parties involved in that shipment.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, court order, or a valid request from government authorities, or to protect the rights and safety of NNK, our drivers, or the public.

5. Cookies and Website Data

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may use essential technical data to deliver and secure the site. You can control or block cookies in your browser settings.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as long as required by law. Some records, such as driver qualification and employment records, must be kept for periods set by federal regulations.

7. Keeping Your Information Safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss. No website or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to limit how we use it, or to withdraw consent you previously gave (including consent to receive text messages). To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to keep certain information when the law requires it.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
