SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 8, 2026

These terms apply to text messages sent by NNK Freight Inc from (331) 295-2115.

1. What We Text About

NNK Freight Inc sends conversational text messages to customers, shipping partners, drivers, and driver applicants. Messages may include shipment and load status, pickup and delivery updates, scheduling, driver application follow-ups, and replies to your questions. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. How to Opt In

You can opt in to receive text messages from us in either of these ways:

  • Text us first: by sending a text message to (331) 295-2115, you agree to receive conversational text replies and related updates from NNK Freight Inc.
  • Tell our team: by giving your mobile number to an NNK team member and agreeing, verbally or in writing, to receive text messages, for example when booking a shipment or applying to drive.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of doing business with us or of applying for a job.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your shipments, application, or conversation with us.

4. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, based on your mobile carrier plan.

5. How to Opt Out

Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving texts. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. You can also opt out by calling (331) 295-2115 or emailing safety@nnkfreightinc.com. To opt back in, text START to (331) 295-2115.

6. Getting Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, call (331) 295-2115, or email safety@nnkfreightinc.com.

7. Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

For more about how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.

8. Carrier Disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
