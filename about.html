About Us

About NNK Freight Inc

Based in Chicago, NNK Freight Inc was started to give shippers something that's harder to find than it should be: a carrier that shows up, follows through, and keeps you in the loop. We're a young company with a hands-on approach, and every load we take gets treated like it's our most important one.

Your Freight, Handled Right

We focus on getting it done correctly the first time, with fast pickups, careful handling, and clean deliveries.

Routes That Reach You

Whether it's a short run around the Chicago area or a haul halfway across the country, we map out each trip to keep your freight moving.

Trucks You Can Count On

Breakdowns cause delays, so we stay ahead of them with routine inspections and regular maintenance on every unit.

Time Matters to Us

Missed appointments cost you money and customers. We plan routes with time to spare and let you know right away if anything on the road changes.

One Load or One Hundred

From a single pallet run to steady weekly lanes, we adapt to your schedule and volume instead of making you fit ours.

Safety Isn't Optional

We stay compliant with DOT regulations and expect the same discipline from every driver and every truck, so your cargo gets there intact.

Real People, Real Answers

When you call NNK, you get someone who knows your shipment, not a phone tree or a ticket number.

Updates Without Asking

We check in from pickup to delivery, so you always know where your load stands without having to chase anyone down.
